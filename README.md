## What is FRICKED?

FRICKED is a mobile app built for SRM students to instantly access their academic data — without logging into the clunky official portal every time.

Clean. Fast. Dark.

---

## Features

**📊 Attendance**
See your attendance percentage per subject. Know exactly how many classes you can skip or need to attend to stay safe.

**📝 Marks**
View your internal assessment marks with a full breakdown per test — all in one tap.

**🗓️ Timetable**
Day-order based schedule so you always know what's next, regardless of the day of the week.

**👤 Profile**
Your student details at a glance.

**🔄 Always Fresh**
Pull to refresh any screen for live data straight from the SRM portal.
